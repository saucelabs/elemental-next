How to Access Previous Tips

Intro​

Elemental Selenium started as a weekly newsletter, and over the years it collected dozens of tips. You found one of them, but how do you find the others that are relevant to you?

A Solution​

Check out the tips page! It lists every tip, newest first, and each card shows the level, the category, and the tags of the tip. Click any of them, or use the filters at the top (order, categories, and levels), to narrow down the list.

Example​

Say you are reading Tip 7, about page objects. At the bottom of the tip you'll find its tags.

Click a tag (e.g., 'page object') to see every tip with that tag.

On the tips page, click the category of a card (e.g., 'TOOLS') to see all the tips in that category.

Expected Behavior​

  • Open the tips page (or the tags of a tip)
  • Click a filtering option
  • See a filtered list of tips
  • View and enjoy another relevant tip

Summary​

Hopefully this has helped turn you onto the bounty of freely available Selenium tips that were right at your fingertips this whole time.

Happy Testing!
