## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The `SetUp` method (lines 15 to 26) starts Chrome and limits its network with `NetworkConditions`: every request
waits half a second (`Latency`), and the browser downloads at most 200 KB per second and uploads at most 50 KB per
second (`DownloadThroughput` and `UploadThroughput`, in bytes per second). The `TearDown` method (lines 28 to 32)
quits the browser.

### The Test

`PageLoadsOnSlowNetwork` (lines 34 to 45) loads the page and measures how long it takes. Then it checks the heading
of the page, to make sure it loaded, and that it took at least 3 seconds. On a fast connection, the page loads in 1
or 2 seconds, so the check shows that the limits work.
