## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The setup is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): the fixture in lines 16 to 36 skips the test without credentials,
starts the sessions with the Sauce Labs options, and ends them after the test. `find` (lines 39 and 40) waits up to 30
seconds for an element to be visible.

### Answering the Pop-up

`allow_if_asked` (lines 43 to 47) waits up to 10 seconds for the button of the pop-up, and taps it. If the pop-up
doesn't show (the permission was granted another way), the wait times out, and the test goes on.

To grant the permissions up front instead, set `options.auto_grant_permissions = True` for Android, or
`options.auto_accept_alerts = True` for iOS.

### The Tests

- Lines 56 to 58 open the Geo Location screen from the menu, and allow the location while using the app. On Android,
  the button belongs to the permission controller of the system, and line 58 finds it by its resource ID.
- Line 59 checks that the Geo Location screen shows.
- Lines 68 to 71 do the same on iOS. The pop-up is an alert of the system, and Appium finds its buttons by their
  labels, like the elements of the app.
