## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`mobilewright.config.ts`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): a project for Android and one
for iOS, on Sauce Labs real devices. On Android, the Sauce Labs driver installs the app with its permissions granted, so
the location pop-up doesn't show there.

### The Test

- Line 4 waits for the catalog, since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Line 5 answers every pop-up of the test. Mobilewright finds system alerts and permission pop-ups while an action or
  an `expect` checks the screen, and calls the handler with a `Dialog`: `accept()` taps its "allow" button,
  `dismiss()` its "don't allow" button, and `tap(name)` any button. `dialog.type()` tells whether it is a permission
  pop-up, and `dialog.isSystem()` whether it comes from the system. Without a handler, Mobilewright leaves pop-ups
  alone.
- Lines 7 to 15 open the Geo Location screen from the menu of each platform, and check that it shows. While the
  `expect` waits for the screen, Mobilewright finds the pop-up, and the handler accepts it.

`screen.waitForEvent('dialog')` waits for the next pop-up instead, for a test that must check that it shows.
