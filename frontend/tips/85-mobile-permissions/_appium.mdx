## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): an Android phone and an iPhone on Sauce
Labs, each with the demo app. To grant the permissions up front instead, add `'appium:autoGrantPermissions': true` to
the Android capabilities, or `'appium:autoAcceptAlerts': true` to the iOS ones.

### The Test

- Lines 5 to 17 pick the selectors of the platform. On Android, the button of the pop-up belongs to the permission
  controller of the system, and `id=` finds it by its resource ID. On iOS, the pop-up is an alert of the system, and
  `~` finds its button by its label.
- Lines 19 and 20 open the Geo Location screen from the menu.
- Lines 21 to 24 wait up to 10 seconds for the "allow" button, and tap it. If the pop-up doesn't show (the permission
  was granted another way), `waitForDisplayed` fails, `catch` turns that into `false`, and the test goes on.
- Line 25 checks that the Geo Location screen shows.
