## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The setup is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): lines 23 to 43 skip the tests without credentials and end the
session after each test, and lines 83 to 89 are the Sauce Labs options. `find` (lines 78 to 81) waits up to 30 seconds
for an element to be visible.

### Answering the Pop-up

`allowIfAsked` (lines 69 to 76) waits up to 10 seconds for the button of the pop-up, and taps it. If the pop-up doesn't
show (the permission was granted another way), the wait times out, and the test goes on.

To grant the permissions up front instead, add `.setAutoGrantPermissions(true)` to the Android options, or
`.setAutoAcceptAlerts(true)` to the iOS options.

### The Tests

- Lines 51 to 53 open the Geo Location screen from the menu, and allow the location while using the app. On Android,
  the button belongs to the permission controller of the system, and line 53 finds it by its resource ID.
- Line 54 checks that the Geo Location screen shows.
- Lines 63 to 66 do the same on iOS. The pop-up is an alert of the system, and Appium finds its buttons by their
  labels, like the elements of the app.
