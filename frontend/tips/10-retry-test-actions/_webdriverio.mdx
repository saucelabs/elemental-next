### The Test

Lines 4 to 10 are the test itself.

`browser.waitUntil` runs the function we give it until it returns `true`, or until the timeout (30 seconds here) runs
out. That makes it a simple way to retry a group of actions.

In the function, we load the page, get the text of the notification, and return whether it says `Action successful`.

If the message never shows up, the test fails with the `timeoutMsg` we set.
