### The Test

Lines 4 to 17 are the test itself.

First, `browser.emulate('clock')` replaces the timers of the page with fake ones, and returns the clock. They don't
move on their own. Then the test loads the page and clicks the button, and the toast shows up with its message.

`clock.tick(59000)` moves the clock forward 59 seconds, and runs what was due (nothing yet): the toast is still there.
One more second runs the callback that hides the toast, and it's gone. `$$` finds all the toasts, and
`toBeElementsArrayOfSize` checks how many there are.
