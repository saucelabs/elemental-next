## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 1 and 2 pull in the `assert` module for the assertions and Vibium's `browser` to drive the browser.

### Setup and Teardown

After creating a `describe` to group our tests, lines 8 to 15 start and stop the browser.

The `beforeEach` method runs before each test. It starts the browser with `browser.start()` and gets its first page
(tab) with `bro.page()`. The `afterEach` method runs after each test and closes the browser with `bro.stop()`.

### The Test

Lines 17 to 31 are the test itself.

First, `page.clock.install()` replaces the timers of the page with fake ones. They don't move on their own. Then the
test loads the page and clicks the button, and the toast shows up with its message.

`page.clock.runFor(59000)` moves the clock forward 59 seconds, and runs what was due (nothing yet): the toast is still
there. One more second runs the callback that hides the toast, and it's gone. The test counts the toasts by running
`document.querySelectorAll(".toast").length` in the page with `evaluate`.
