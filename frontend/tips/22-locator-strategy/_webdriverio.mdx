### The Tests

Each test finds a link with a CSS selector, prints its `href` attribute, and checks it.

In WebdriverIO 10, `$` is strict: it fails when the selector matches more than one element, so a vague selector can't
silently pick the wrong one. Here every selector matches many links, and we want the first one, so we use `$$`
(all the matches) and take the first with `[0]`.

- Lines 8 to 12 use `a`, which matches every link on the page. The first one is the "Fork me on GitHub" banner, not
  what we're after.
- Lines 14 to 18 scope the selector to the content of the page (`#content a`). The first match is a download link.
- Lines 20 to 24 scope it further, to the example (`#content .example a`). It's still a download link, and the
  selector says more clearly what we want.

The more specific the selector, the less likely it is to break or to find the wrong element when the page changes.
