### The Tests

`geolocation` (lines 4 to 14) sets the location to Berlin with `browser.emulate('geolocation', ...)` and loads the
page. `setPermissions` grants the page the geolocation permission, so the browser doesn't ask the user. It applies to
the origin of the current page, so the test calls it after loading the page.

Then the test loads the page and clicks the button. The page asks the browser for the location, and shows the
latitude and longitude of Berlin. `toHaveText` waits for them and checks them.

`time zone and locale` (lines 16 to 27) sets the time zone with `browser.emulate('timezone', ...)` and the language
with `browser.emulate('locale', ...)`. WebdriverIO does both with WebDriver BiDi.

Then the test loads the page. The server greets the user in German (`Hallo!`), and the page shows the language
(`de-DE`), the time zone (`Asia/Tokyo`), the date in German at the time of Tokyo (`Mittwoch, 15. April 2026 um
21:00`), and the number with German separators (`1.234.567,891`).
