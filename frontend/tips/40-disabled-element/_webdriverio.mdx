### Importing Libraries

Line 1 pulls in the WebdriverIO globals (`browser`, `$` and `expect`).

### The Test

Lines 4 to 9 are the test itself.

After visiting the page, we find each option by its text. WebdriverIO has a selector for that: `tag=text` finds the
element with that tag and that exact text (e.g., `option=Option 1`), so we don't need to loop over the options.

Then `toBeDisabled` checks that the first option (`Please select an option`) is disabled, and `toBeEnabled` checks
that `Option 1` is enabled.
