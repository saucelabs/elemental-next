## Code Walkthrough

### Listening to Selenide

With Selenium, we wrap the driver with an `EventFiringDecorator` to know about each action. Selenide already logs
every step it takes (opening a page, clicking, checking, ...), and it tells the listeners we give it. That's how its
reports and integrations (e.g., Allure) work.

### The Listener

`Wrapper` implements Selenide's `LogEventListener`:

- Lines 10 to 19 keep the messages in a list (so the test can check them), and print them.
- Lines 21 to 24 (`beforeEvent`) log each step before it runs, with the element (or `open`, or `webdriver`) and what is
  done with it.
- Lines 26 to 29 (`afterEvent`) log each step after it runs, with its status (`PASS` or `FAIL`).

### The Test

Lines 20 to 29 of `WrapperTest` add the listener before each test (`SelenideLogger.addListener`), with a name, and
remove it by that name after the test.

Lines 33 to 35 open the home page, click the `Checkboxes` link, and check the new URL. Lines 37 to 45 check what the
listener logged. Note that the first `open` also starts the browser (`webdriver create()`): Selenide opens it when we
first need it.
