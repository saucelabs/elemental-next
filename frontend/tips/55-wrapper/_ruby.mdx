## Code Walkthrough

### The Listener

`Wrapper` inherits from `Selenium::WebDriver::Support::AbstractEventListener` and overrides the hooks we're
interested in: `after_navigate_to`, `before_find`, `after_find`, `before_click`, and `after_click`. Each of them
receives the arguments of the action (e.g., the URL, or the locator) and the driver. They log a message with `log`,
which outputs it and keeps it in the `messages` list.

### The Test

The `setup` method creates our listener and passes it to Selenium when creating the driver
(`Selenium::WebDriver.for(:firefox, listener: @listener)`). From then on, every action calls the listener's hooks.

The test visits the page, clicks the link, and asserts the messages the listener logged.
