### Sorting by Amount

The dues are texts like `$50.00`. Lines 16 and 17 hold a comparator that sorts them by their amount, so `$100.00`
comes after `$51.00`.

### Setup

### The Tests

Each test does the same steps:

1. find the cells of a column with `$$` (all the elements that match a CSS selector),
2. take their texts and sort them to know the order we expect,
3. click the header of the column to sort the table,
4. check that the cells have the texts in that order.

The check with `shouldHave(exactTexts(...))` waits until the table is sorted, so we don't need to wait for the
sorting ourselves.

- Lines 25 to 30 sort the dues of the first table (the 4th column, `td:nth-of-type(4)`) in ascending order.
- Lines 33 to 39 click the header twice to sort them in descending order.
- Lines 42 to 47 sort the emails (the 3rd column) in alphabetical order.
- Lines 50 to 55 do the first test on the second table, which has a class on each cell (e.g., `.dues`). These
  locators are easier to read, and they keep working if the columns change order.
