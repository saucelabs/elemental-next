## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): an Android phone and an iPhone on Sauce
Labs, each with the demo app. The device names ask for phones with a regular screen (`Google Pixel ([89]|1[0-9])( Pro)?`
and `iPhone 1[4-7].*`), since the test taps "Add to cart" without scrolling: on a small iPhone SE, or on the inner
screen of a foldable, the button is below the fold.

### The Test

In WebdriverIO, a selector that starts with `~` finds an element by its accessibility ID: the content description on
Android, and the accessibility identifier on iOS.

- Lines 5 to 17 pick the IDs of the platform. The Android and iOS apps use different names for the same elements.
- Lines 19 to 21 tap the first product of the catalog, once it is displayed. Every product has the same ID (the image on
  Android, the product item on iOS), so `$$` finds all of them and `[0]` takes the first one. `$` fails when more than
  one element matches, as a safety net against selectors that are not specific enough.
- Lines 22 and 23 tap "Add to cart" and open the cart. WebdriverIO waits for each element to exist before tapping it.
- Line 24 checks that the checkout button is displayed, waiting for it if needed.
