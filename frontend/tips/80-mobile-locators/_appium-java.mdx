## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

Lines 22 to 41 and 76 to 82 are the setup from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): the Sauce Labs URL, the demo apps, skipping
the tests without credentials, ending the session after each test, and the Sauce Labs options.

### Picking the Devices

The test taps "Add to cart" without scrolling, so it needs a phone with a regular screen. On a small iPhone SE, or on
the inner screen of a foldable, the button is below the fold. The device names ask for those phones only: line 46 asks
for `Google Pixel [0-9]+( Pro)?` (a Pixel or a Pixel Pro, no "a", "XL" or "Fold" models) and line 60 for `iPhone
1[4-7].*` (an iPhone 14 to 17). Scrolling to an element is the topic of another tip.

### Finding Elements

`find` (lines 71 to 74) finds an element by its accessibility ID, with `AppiumBy.accessibilityId`. It waits up to 30
seconds for the element to be visible, since each tap opens a new screen. On Android, Appium matches the content
description of the element, and on iOS its accessibility identifier.

### The Tests

- Lines 51 to 54 add the backpack to the cart on Android. `find("Product Image")` returns the first element with that
  ID, the image of the first product of the catalog. Then the test taps "Add to cart" (`Tap to add product to cart`),
  opens the cart (`View cart`), and checks that the checkout button (`Confirms products for checkout`) is displayed.
- Lines 65 to 68 do the same on iOS. The iOS app uses other names for the same elements: `ProductItem` (a product
  of the catalog), `AddToCart`, `Cart-tab-item` and `ProceedToCheckout`.
