## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

Lines 10 to 35 are the setup from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): the Sauce Labs URL, the demo apps, and a fixture that
skips the test without credentials, starts the sessions with the Sauce Labs options, and ends them after the test.

### Picking the Devices

The test taps "Add to cart" without scrolling, so it needs a phone with a regular screen. On a small iPhone SE, or on
the inner screen of a foldable, the button is below the fold. The device names ask for those phones only: line 45 asks
for `Google Pixel [0-9]+( Pro)?` (a Pixel or a Pixel Pro, no "a", "XL" or "Fold" models) and line 57 for `iPhone
1[4-7].*` (an iPhone 14 to 17). Scrolling to an element is the topic of another tip.

### Finding Elements

`find` (lines 38 to 40) finds an element by its accessibility ID, with `AppiumBy.ACCESSIBILITY_ID`. It waits up to 30
seconds for the element to be visible, since each tap opens a new screen. On Android, Appium matches the content
description of the element, and on iOS its accessibility identifier.

### The Tests

- Lines 49 to 52 add the backpack to the cart on Android. `find(driver, 'Product Image')` returns the first element
  with that ID, the image of the first product of the catalog. Then the test taps "Add to cart"
  (`Tap to add product to cart`), opens the cart (`View cart`), and checks that the checkout button
  (`Confirms products for checkout`) is displayed.
- Lines 61 to 64 do the same on iOS. The iOS app uses other names for the same elements: `ProductItem` (a product
  of the catalog), `AddToCart`, `Cart-tab-item` and `ProceedToCheckout`.
