### The Test

Lines 4 to 11 are the test itself.

After visiting the page, `dragAndDrop` drags the first column (`#column-a`) and drops it on the second one
(`#column-b`). WebdriverIO handles HTML5 drag and drop in Chrome, so we don't need the JavaScript helper (`dnd.js`)
that the Selenium examples inject into the page.

Then we check that the columns swapped: the first column now shows `B`, and the second one shows `A`.
