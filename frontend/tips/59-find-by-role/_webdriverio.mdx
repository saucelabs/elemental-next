### The Test

The test loads the page, and `$('aria/Fruit')` finds the first element whose accessible name is "Fruit": the combobox
(the list of options has the same name, but comes after it). The test clicks it, and the list of fruits opens. Then
`$('aria/Mango')` finds the option, and the test clicks it. At the end, `toHaveText` checks that the combobox shows
"Mango", and that the page shows `Selected: mango`.

The `aria/` selector matches the name only, not the role. When two elements have the same name, use a more specific
locator for the one you want.
