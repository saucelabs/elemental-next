## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The `SetUp` method (lines 23 to 30) turns on WebDriver BiDi with `UseWebSocketUrl`, starts Chrome, and connects to
BiDi with `AsBiDiAsync`. The `TearDown` method (lines 32 to 40) closes the BiDi connection and quits the browser.

### The Test

`RecordUserJourney` (lines 42 to 84) subscribes to `Network.BeforeRequestSent` (lines 47 to 59), which the browser
sends before each request. The handler skips the requests to other sites, and keeps the method, the URL and the start time of the others.
Then the test goes from the home page to [the second Dynamic Loading example](https://the-internet.herokuapp.com/dynamic_loading/2),
clicks Start, and waits until "Hello World!" shows up.

At the end, it writes the HAR file, `journey.har`, with `System.Text.Json` (lines 69 to 78), and checks that the three
pages are in it.
