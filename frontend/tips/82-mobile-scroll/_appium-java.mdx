### Swiping

`swipeUp` (lines 86 to 96) swipes up once, with the W3C actions of Selenium:

- Lines 87 and 88 get the size of the screen, and the middle of it.
- Line 89 creates a pointer of the `TOUCH` kind: a finger.
- Lines 90 to 94 are the gesture: move the finger to 70% of the height, touch the screen, move to 30% of the height in
  half a second, and lift the finger. The content scrolls up by about 40% of the screen.
- Line 95 sends the gesture to the device.

`swipeUpUntilDisplayed` (lines 76 to 84) checks for the element before each swipe, and swipes up to 20 times. It looks
for the element with `findElements`, which returns an empty list instead of failing when the element is not there yet.
It returns `true` as soon as the element is displayed.

### The Tests

- Line 56 waits for the catalog, since the app shows a splash screen first (`waitForCatalog`, lines 72 to 74).
- Line 57 finds the onesie by its text, with a UiAutomator selector, and line 58 swipes until it is displayed.
- Lines 67 to 69 do the same on iOS, with an iOS predicate on the label of the onesie.
