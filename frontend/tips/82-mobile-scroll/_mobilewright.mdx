## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`mobilewright.config.ts`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): a project for Android and one
for iOS, on Sauce Labs real devices.

### The Test

- Line 4 waits for the catalog, since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Line 5 finds the onesie by its text.
- Lines 7 to 9 swipe up until the onesie is visible, up to 20 times. `screen.swipe('up')` swipes from the middle of
  the screen, by half its height. `duration: 1000` makes it a slow swipe: the demo app ignores faster ones on Android.
  `isVisible()` tells whether the element is on screen right now, without waiting.
- Line 10 checks that the onesie is visible.

Mobilewright also has `onesie.scrollIntoViewIfNeeded()`, which does this loop for you. Its swipes are fast, and they
don't scroll the catalog of the demo app on Android, so the test swipes on its own.
