# How to Scroll to an Element in a Mobile App

## Intro

On a phone, most screens don't fit: a long list, a form, a product page with its button at the bottom. In a web page,
WebDriver scrolls to an element before clicking it. In a mobile app, an element below the fold is often not there at
all: Android and iOS lists only create the rows on screen, and reuse them as you scroll. So the test can't find the
element until it scrolls, and it can't scroll to an element it can't find.

## A Solution

Swipe, as a user would, until the element shows up. Swipe a bit at a time, check for the element after each swipe, and
give up after a few swipes, so a missing element fails the test instead of swiping forever.

- **Appium** swipes with W3C actions: a finger touches the screen, moves up, and lifts. The Appium drivers also have
  their own commands (`mobile: scrollGesture` on Android, `mobile: scroll` on iOS), and WebdriverIO has
  `scrollIntoView`, but a plain swipe works the same on both platforms and in every app.
- **Mobilewright** has `screen.swipe()`. Its `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded()` does the loop for you in simple screens.
- **Espresso** has `swipeUp()` on a view, and `scrollTo()` for a view inside a `ScrollView`.
- **XCUITest** has `swipeUp()` on the app or on an element.

On Android, the catalog of the demo app is a list inside a scroll view, and only the outer scroll view moves. Swipe on
the screen, or on the view that scrolls: a swipe on the inner list does nothing. Also swipe slowly enough (half a
second or more): a fast swipe can turn into a fling the app ignores, or scroll past the element.

The examples use Sauce Labs' demo app ("My Demo App", for [Android](https://github.com/saucelabs/my-demo-app-android)
and [iOS](https://github.com/saucelabs/my-demo-app-ios)). Its catalog has more than thirty products, and the test swipes
through it until the "Sauce Labs Onesie" shows up, near the end of the list.

## Expected Behavior

- Sauce Labs finds a free real device and installs the demo app
- The test waits for the catalog, which shows after the splash screen
- It swipes up through the catalog until the "Sauce Labs Onesie" is on screen, up to ten swipes
- It checks that the onesie is displayed

## Summary

Swipe in small steps, check after each one, and stop after a few tries. Your test then finds elements below the fold on
any screen size, and fails fast when an element is really missing.

Happy Testing!
