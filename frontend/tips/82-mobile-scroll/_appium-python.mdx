### Setup and Teardown

### Swiping

`swipe_up` (lines 53 to 61) swipes up once, with the W3C actions of Selenium:

- Lines 54 and 55 get the size of the screen, and the middle of it.
- Line 56 creates the actions with a pointer of the touch kind (a finger), and moves that last half a second
  (`duration=500`).
- Lines 57 to 60 are the gesture: move the finger to 70% of the height, touch the screen, move to 30% of the height,
  and lift the finger. The content scrolls up by about 40% of the screen.
- Line 61 sends the gesture to the device.

`swipe_up_until_displayed` (lines 45 to 50) checks for the element before each swipe, and swipes up to 20 times. It
looks for the element with `find_elements`, which returns an empty list instead of failing when the element is not
there yet. It returns `True` as soon as the element is displayed.

### The Tests

- Line 70 waits for the catalog, since the app shows a splash screen first (`wait_for_catalog`, lines 41 and 42).
- Line 71 finds the onesie by its text, with a UiAutomator selector, and line 72 swipes until it is displayed.
- Lines 81 to 83 do the same on iOS, with an iOS predicate on the label of the onesie.
