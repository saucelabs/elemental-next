## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): an Android phone and an iPhone on Sauce
Labs, each with the demo app.

### Swiping

`swipeUp` (lines 3 to 12) swipes up once, with `driver.action`, the W3C actions of WebdriverIO:

- Lines 4 and 5 get the size of the screen, and the middle of it.
- Line 6 creates a pointer of the `touch` type: a finger.
- Lines 7 to 11 are the gesture: move the finger to 70% of the height, touch the screen, move to 30% of the height in
  half a second, and lift the finger. The content scrolls up by about 40% of the screen.

### The Test

- Lines 16 to 19 pick the locators of the platform: the first product, and the onesie (a UiAutomator selector on
  Android, an iOS predicate on iOS).
- Line 21 waits for the catalog, since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Lines 22 to 24 swipe until the onesie is displayed, up to 20 times. `isDisplayed` returns `false` instead of failing
  when the element is not there yet.
- Line 25 checks that the onesie is displayed.
