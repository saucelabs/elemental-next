### Accepting and Dismissing

Selenide has methods for JavaScript dialogs. They wait for the dialog, so we don't need a `WebDriverWait`:

- In lines 21 to 24, `confirm()` accepts the confirmation (like clicking `OK`), and the page says
  `You clicked: Ok`.
- In lines 27 to 30, `dismiss()` dismisses it (like clicking `Cancel`), and the page says `You clicked: Cancel`.

Both methods return the text of the dialog, and they can also check it: `confirm("I am a JS Confirm")` fails if the
text is different.
