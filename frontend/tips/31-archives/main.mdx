# How to Access Previous Tips

## Intro

Elemental Selenium started as a weekly newsletter, and over the years it collected dozens of tips. You found one
of them, but how do you find the others that are relevant to you?

## A Solution

Check out [the tips page](/tips)! It lists every tip, newest first, and each card shows the level, the category, and
the tags of the tip. Click any of them, or use the filters at the top (order, categories, and levels), to narrow down
the list.

## Example

Say you are reading [Tip 7](/tips/7-use-a-page-object), about page objects. At the bottom of the tip you'll find its
tags.

![tags at the bottom of tip 7](./../images/in_tip.png)

Click a tag (e.g., 'page object') to see every tip with that tag.

![tips tagged with page object](./../images/list_tag.png)

On [the tips page](/tips), click the category of a card (e.g., 'TOOLS') to see all the tips in that category.

![tips in the tools category](./../images/list_category.png)

## Expected Behavior

- Open the tips page (or the tags of a tip)
- Click a filtering option
- See a filtered list of tips
- View and enjoy another relevant tip

## Summary

Hopefully this has helped turn you onto the bounty of freely available Selenium tips that were right at your fingertips
this whole time.

Happy Testing!
