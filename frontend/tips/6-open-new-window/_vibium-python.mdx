## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 1 and 2 pull in `pytest` to run the test, and Vibium's `browser` to drive the browser.

### Setup and Teardown

Lines 5 to 9 are a pytest fixture that starts the browser, gives it to the test, and closes it after the test. The
test needs the browser itself (not only a page), to open a new tab.

### The Test

Lines 12 to 20 are the test itself.

We visit a page in the first tab. Then `bro.new_page()` opens a new tab and gives it to us as a page object, and we
visit another page in it. There is no switching: each tab is its own page object.

Finally, we check that the browser has two tabs, and the heading of the new one.

### Executing the Test

Before executing the test, we need to make sure the required libraries are declared on the `requirements.txt` file.
