## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 3 to 10 pull in Vibium's classes to drive the browser, and JUnit to run the test.

### Setup and Teardown

Lines 13 and 14 declare the browser and the page. The `beforeEach` method (lines 16 to 20) starts the browser and
gets its first page (tab). The `afterEach` method (lines 22 to 27) closes the browser.

### The Test

Lines 29 to 38 are the test itself.

We visit a page in the first tab. Then `browser.newPage()` opens a new tab and gives it to us as a `Page`, and we
visit another page in it. There is no switching: each tab is its own `Page`.

Finally, we check that the browser has two tabs, and the heading of the new one.
