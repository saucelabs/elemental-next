## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The fixture (lines 13 to 29) skips the test without credentials, starts a session on an iPhone with the Sauce Labs
options, and ends it after the test, as in [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile). The Android demo app runs in portrait only, so
this example uses iOS.

### The Test

- Lines 33 to 35 wait for the first product of the catalog (`ProductItem`), since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Line 36 checks that the device starts in portrait: `driver.orientation` is `'PORTRAIT'` or `'LANDSCAPE'`.
- Line 38 rotates the device to landscape, by setting `driver.orientation`.
- Lines 39 and 40 check the orientation, and that the product is still visible.
- Lines 42 and 43 rotate back to portrait and check it.
