## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`mobilewright.config.ts`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile), with the iOS project only: the
Android demo app runs in portrait only. `npm test` runs `mobilewright test`.

### The Test

- Line 3 gets the `device` fixture, which controls the device, and the `screen`.
- Lines 4 and 5 wait for the first product of the catalog (`ProductItem`), since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Line 6 checks that the device starts in portrait.
- Line 8 rotates the device to landscape with `device.setOrientation()`.
- Lines 9 and 10 check the orientation, and that the product is still visible.
- Lines 12 and 13 rotate back to portrait and check it.
