### Setup and Teardown

Lines 26 to 37 skip the test without credentials and end the session after it, as in [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile). Lines
41 to 47 start a session on an iPhone, with the Sauce Labs options. The Android demo app runs in portrait only, so this
example uses iOS.

### The Test

- Lines 49 to 51 wait for the first product of the catalog (`ProductItem`), since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Line 52 checks that the device starts in portrait. `getOrientation` comes from `SupportsRotation`, which both
  `IOSDriver` and `AndroidDriver` implement.
- Line 54 rotates the device to landscape.
- Lines 55 and 56 check the orientation, and that the product is still visible.
- Lines 58 and 59 rotate back to portrait and check it.
