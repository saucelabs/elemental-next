## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile), with the iPhone only: the Android demo
app runs in portrait only.

### The Test

- Lines 5 and 6 wait for the first product of the catalog (`ProductItem`), since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Line 7 checks that the device starts in portrait.
- Line 9 rotates the device to landscape with `setOrientation`.
- Lines 10 and 11 check the orientation, and that the product is still displayed.
- Lines 13 and 14 rotate back to portrait and check it.
