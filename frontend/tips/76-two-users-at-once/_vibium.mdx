## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 1 and 2 pull in `crypto` (for the room name) and Vibium's `browser`.

### The Steps of a User

Line 5 builds the URL of the chat with a random room.

- `join` (lines 7 to 14) opens the chat in the browser of the user, waits until the page is connected
  (`waitForFunction` waits until the expression is true), types the name, clicks "Join", and returns the page.
- `sendMessage` (lines 16 to 19) types the message and clicks "Send", on the page of the user.

### One Browser for Each User

The `beforeEach` hook (lines 25 to 28) starts one browser for Alice and one for Bob, and the `afterEach` hook (lines
30 to 33) stops both.

### The Test

Alice and Bob join (lines 36 and 37). Messages are not saved, so Alice waits until she sees "Bob joined" before she
writes (line 39). Then Alice sends "Hi Bob", and Bob's page should show it (lines 41 and 42), and the other way around
(lines 44 and 45). `find({text: ...})` inside `#messages` waits until an element with that text shows up, and fails
the test when it doesn't.
