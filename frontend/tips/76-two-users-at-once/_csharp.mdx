## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 1 to 5 pull in `System` (for `Guid` and `TimeSpan`), MSTest, Selenium, and its support library (for
`WebDriverWait`).

### One Browser for Each User

Lines 13 and 14 build the URL of the chat with a random room. The `SetUp` method (lines 18 to 23) starts one Firefox
for Alice and one for Bob, and the `TearDown` method (lines 25 to 30) quits both.

### The Steps of a User

- `WaitForText` (lines 32 to 36) waits up to 10 seconds until the text of an element contains the given text.
- `Join` (lines 38 to 44) opens the chat, waits until the page is connected, types the name, and clicks "Join".
- `SendMessage` (lines 46 to 50) types the message and clicks "Send".

Each one takes the driver of the user, so the same steps work for Alice and for Bob.

### The Test

Alice and Bob join (lines 55 and 56). Messages are not saved, so Alice waits until she sees "Bob joined" before she
writes (line 58). Then Alice sends "Hi Bob", and the test waits until Bob's page shows it (lines 60 and 61), and the
other way around (lines 63 and 64). When the text doesn't show up, the wait fails the test.
