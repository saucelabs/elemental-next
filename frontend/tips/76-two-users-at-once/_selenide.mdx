## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 3 to 11 pull in the Selenide conditions (`exactText` and `text`), `SelenideConfig` and `SelenideDriver`, `UUID`
(for the room name), and JUnit.

### One Browser for Each User

Lines 15 and 16 build the URL of the chat with a random room. The static `open()` and `$()` of Selenide drive one
browser. For two users, the `setUp` method (lines 20 to 25) creates two `SelenideDriver` objects: each one has its own
configuration (here, waits of up to 10 seconds) and its own browser, and has the same `open()` and `$()` methods. The
`tearDown` method (lines 27 to 31) closes both.

### The Steps of a User

- `join` (lines 33 to 38) opens the chat, waits until the page is connected (`exactText`), types the name, and clicks
  "Join".
- `sendMessage` (lines 40 to 43) types the message and clicks "Send".

Each one takes the `SelenideDriver` of the user, so the same steps work for Alice and for Bob.

### The Test

Alice and Bob join (lines 47 and 48). Messages are not saved, so Alice waits until she sees "Bob joined" before she
writes (line 50). Then Alice sends "Hi Bob", and Bob's page should have it (lines 52 and 53), and the other way around
(lines 55 and 56). `shouldHave` waits until the condition is met, and fails the test when it isn't.
