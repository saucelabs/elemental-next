## Code Walkthrough

### Importing Libraries

Lines 3 to 10 pull in Vibium (with its `SelectorOptions` type), `UUID` (for the room name), and JUnit.

### One Browser for Each User

Lines 14 and 15 build the URL of the chat with a random room. The `beforeEach` method (lines 19 to 23) starts one
browser for Alice and one for Bob, and the `afterEach` method (lines 25 to 33) stops both.

### The Steps of a User

- `join` (lines 35 to 42) opens the chat in the browser of the user, waits until the page is connected
  (`waitForFunction` waits until the expression is true), types the name, clicks "Join", and returns the page.
- `sendMessage` (lines 44 to 47) types the message and clicks "Send", on the page of the user.

### The Test

Alice and Bob join (lines 51 and 52). Messages are not saved, so Alice waits until she sees "Bob joined" before she
writes (line 54). Then Alice sends "Hi Bob", and Bob's page should show it (lines 56 and 57), and the other way around
(lines 59 and 60). `find(new SelectorOptions().text(...))` inside `#messages` waits until an element with that text
shows up, and fails the test when it doesn't.
