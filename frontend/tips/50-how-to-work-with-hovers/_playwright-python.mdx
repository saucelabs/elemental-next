### The Test

Lines 4 to 11 are the test. The page has a few avatars, and hovering over one shows a caption with a link.

We find the first avatar and the caption inside it. Before hovering, the caption is hidden (`to_be_hidden`). Then
`hover` moves the mouse over the avatar, and the caption becomes visible (`to_be_visible`).
