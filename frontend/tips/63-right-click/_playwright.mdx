## Code Walkthrough

### The Test

Lines 3 to 15 are the test. Right clicking the box on the page opens a JavaScript alert with a message.

The alert blocks the page until someone closes it, and the click doesn't end until then. So before the click, we
listen for the `dialog` event once (lines 6 to 11): the listener saves the message and accepts the alert. Then we
right click with `click({button: 'right'})` and check the message.
