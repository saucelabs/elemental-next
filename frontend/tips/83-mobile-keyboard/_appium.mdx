## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): an Android phone and an iPhone on Sauce
Labs, each with the demo app.

### The Test

- Lines 7 to 21 pick the selectors of the platform. On Android, `id=urlET` finds the URL field by its resource ID. On
  iOS, it has no accessibility ID, and `XCUIElementTypeTextField` finds it by its type, the only text field of the
  screen.
- Lines 23 and 24 open the menu and the WebView screen.
- Lines 26 to 28 type the URL with `setValue` (it clears the field first), and check that the field holds it with
  `toHaveText`.
- Lines 29 to 31 close the keyboard on Android, if it is up. The iOS app doesn't close its keyboard, and "Go To Site"
  is above it.
- Lines 33 and 34 tap "Go To Site" and check that the web view shows up. On iOS, `$$(...)[0]` takes the first match:
  the button and its label share the same name, and the web view screen has more than one web view.
