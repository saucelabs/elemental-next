## Code Walkthrough

### Configuring Sauce Labs

In WebdriverIO, where the browser runs is part of the configuration, not of the test.

<TipCode codePath={"28-download-from-a-remote-browser/code/webdriverio/wdio.conf.ts"} language={"ts"}/>

- Lines 3 to 5 send the tests to Sauce Labs: the credentials, and the region of the data center.
- Lines 7 to 9 add the Sauce service, which names each job and marks it as passed or failed on Sauce Labs.
- Lines 12 to 18 ask for the latest Chrome on Windows 11. `se:downloadsEnabled: true` asks Sauce Labs to keep the
  files the browser downloads.
- Lines 24 to 29 skip the tests when the Sauce Labs credentials are not set.

To use a Selenium Grid instead, point WebdriverIO to its address (`protocol`, `hostname`, `port` and `path`), as in
[tip 52](/tips/52-how-to-use-selenium-grid), and keep `se:downloadsEnabled: true`.

### The Test

The `before` hook (lines 9 to 11) creates a temp folder on our machine, and the `after` hook (lines 13 to 15) deletes
it.

The test (lines 17 to 30) visits the page, saves the name of the first download link, and clicks it.

- Lines 24 and 25 wait up to 10 seconds until the file shows up in `getDownloadableFiles()`, the list of files the
  browser downloaded on the remote machine.
- Lines 28 and 29 copy the file with `downloadFile(fileName, targetDir)` to the temp folder, and check that it is not
  empty.

we need to make sure the required dependencies are declared on the `package.json` file.
