## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile), with phones with a regular screen (a Pixel
8 or newer, and an iPhone 14 to 17), where "Add to cart" shows without scrolling. Its `waitforTimeout: 30000` is how
long every wait lasts at most.

### Waiting

WebdriverIO waits for an element to exist before each action on it. The `waitFor` commands wait for more than that:

- Lines 5 to 19 pick the accessibility IDs of the platform.
- Lines 21 to 23 wait for the first product to be displayed (`waitForDisplayed`), and tap it. The app shows a splash
  screen first, and it can take a few seconds on some devices.
- Lines 25 and 26 wait for "Add to cart" to be enabled (`waitForEnabled`), since the product screen slides in after the
  tap. Line 27 opens the cart.
- Line 29 waits for the checkout button of the cart.
- Line 30 removes the backpack (on iOS, the button and its label share the ID, so `$$(...)[0]` takes the first one).
  Line 31 waits for the checkout button to go away: `reverse: true` turns the wait around.

There is no `browser.pause`: each wait ends as soon as the app is ready.
