## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The setup is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): the fixture in lines 15 to 35 skips the test without credentials,
starts the sessions with the Sauce Labs options, and ends them after the test. Lines 44 and 61 ask for phones with a
regular screen (a Pixel 8 or newer, and an iPhone 14 to 17), where "Add to cart" shows without scrolling. `by_id`
(lines 38 and 39) builds a locator by accessibility ID.

### Waiting

Line 47 creates a `WebDriverWait` that checks a condition again and again, for up to 30 seconds, and returns as soon as
it is true. The conditions come from Selenium's `expected_conditions` (imported as `ec`). Each step of the test waits
for what it needs:

- Line 49 waits for the first product to be visible (`visibility_of_element_located`). The app shows a splash screen
  first, and it can take a few seconds on some devices. `until` returns the element, so the test taps it right away.
- Lines 50 and 51 wait for "Add to cart" and for the cart button to be clickable (`element_to_be_clickable`: visible
  and enabled), since the product screen slides in after the tap.
- Lines 53 and 54 wait for the checkout button of the cart.
- Line 55 removes the backpack, and line 56 waits for the checkout button to go away
  (`invisibility_of_element_located`). The cart is empty, so the app hides it.

Lines 66 to 73 do the same on iOS, with the accessibility IDs of the iOS app.

There is no `time.sleep` and no implicit wait: each wait ends as soon as the app is ready.
