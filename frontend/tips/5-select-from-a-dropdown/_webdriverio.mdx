### The Test

Lines 4 to 10 are the test itself.

After visiting the page, we call `selectByVisibleText` on the dropdown to pick `Option 1`. WebdriverIO also has
`selectByAttribute` (e.g., by value) and `selectByIndex`.

To check the selection, we find the selected option with the `:checked` CSS pseudo-class and check its text.
`toHaveText` waits until it matches.
