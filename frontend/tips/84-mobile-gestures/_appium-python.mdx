## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The setup is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): the fixture in lines 18 to 38 skips the test without credentials,
starts the sessions with the Sauce Labs options, and ends them after the test. Line 66 grants every permission the app
asks for when Sauce Labs installs it (`auto_grant_permissions`), so the Drawing screen opens without a pop-up on
Android.

### The Gesture

`draw_line` (lines 45 to 52) drags a finger across the middle of the pad, with the W3C actions of Selenium:

- Line 46 is the middle of the pad.
- Line 47 creates the actions with a pointer of the touch kind (a finger), and moves that last half a second.
- Lines 48 to 51 are the steps of the finger: move to a quarter of the width, touch the screen, move to three quarters
  of the width, and lift. For a long press, add a pause between the touch and the lift. For a pinch, add a second
  finger (`actions.add_pointer_input`).
- Line 52 sends the gesture to the device.

### Checking the Result

`assert_draws_on` (lines 55 to 59) takes a screenshot of the pad (`screenshot_as_png` on the element), draws the line,
takes another one, and checks that they are different.

### The Tests

- Lines 69 to 71 open the Drawing screen from the menu, and draw on the pad (`Pad to draw on`).
- Lines 80 to 82 do the same on iOS, where the pad is the image `DrawingBackground Icons`. The drawing covers that
  image, so Appium reports it as not visible: `find` (lines 41 and 42) waits for the element to be there
  (`presence_of_element_located`), not to be visible.
