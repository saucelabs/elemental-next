## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`mobilewright.config.ts`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): a project for Android and one
for iOS, on Sauce Labs real devices. On Android, the Sauce Labs driver installs the app with its permissions granted,
so the Drawing screen opens without a pop-up.

### The Test

- Line 4 waits for the catalog, since the app shows a splash screen first.
- Lines 5 to 11 open the Drawing screen from the menu of each platform.
- Line 12 finds the pad by its accessibility label.
- Line 14 takes a screenshot of the pad (`screenshot` on the locator).
- Line 15 drags a finger from the middle of the pad to the right, in one second: `swipe` on a locator starts at its
  center. For other paths, `screen.gesture()` takes the points of each finger, with a time for each one.
- Lines 16 and 17 take another screenshot, and check that it is different.
