## Code Walkthrough

The configuration (`wdio.conf.js`) is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile), with `appium:autoGrantPermissions` for
Android: it grants every permission the app asks for when Sauce Labs installs it, so the Drawing screen opens without a
pop-up.

### The Gesture

`drawLine` (lines 3 to 13) drags a finger across the middle of the pad, with `driver.action`, the W3C actions of
WebdriverIO:

- Lines 4 to 6 get the position and size of the pad, and its middle.
- Line 7 creates a pointer of the `touch` type: a finger.
- Lines 8 to 12 are the steps of the finger: move to a quarter of the width, touch the screen, move to three quarters
  of the width in half a second, and lift. For a long press, add `.pause(1000)` between `down` and `up`.

### The Test

- Lines 17 to 19 pick the selectors of the platform.
- Lines 21 to 24 open the Drawing screen from the menu, and wait for the pad. On iOS, the drawing covers the pad image,
  so the test waits for it to exist (`waitForExist`), not to be displayed.
- Lines 26 to 29 take a screenshot of the pad (`takeScreenshot` on the element), draw the line, take another one, and
  check that they are different.
