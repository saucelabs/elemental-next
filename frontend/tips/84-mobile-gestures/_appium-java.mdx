## Code Walkthrough

### Setup and Teardown

The setup is the one from [tip 30](/tips/30-mobile): lines 30 to 50 skip the tests without credentials and end the
session after each test, and lines 102 to 108 are the Sauce Labs options. Line 57 grants every permission the app asks
for when Sauce Labs installs it (`autoGrantPermissions`), so the Drawing screen opens without a pop-up on Android.

### The Gesture

`drawLine` (lines 84 to 95) drags a finger across the middle of the pad, with the W3C actions of Selenium:

- Lines 85 to 87 compute the middle of the pad (`y`), and where the line starts and ends (a quarter and three quarters
  of its width).
- Line 88 creates a pointer of the `TOUCH` kind: a finger.
- Lines 89 to 93 are the steps of the finger: move to the start, touch the screen, move to the end in half a second,
  and lift. For a long press, add a pause between the touch and the lift. For a pinch, perform two sequences, one for
  each finger.
- Line 94 sends the gesture to the device.

### Checking the Result

`assertDrawsOn` (lines 77 to 82) takes a screenshot of the pad (`getScreenshotAs` on the element), draws the line, takes
another one, and checks that they are different.

### The Tests

- Lines 61 to 63 open the Drawing screen from the menu, and draw on the pad (`Pad to draw on`).
- Lines 72 to 74 do the same on iOS, where the pad is the image `DrawingBackground Icons`. The drawing covers that
  image, so Appium reports it as not visible: `find` (lines 97 to 100) waits for the element to be there
  (`presenceOfElementLocated`), not to be visible.
